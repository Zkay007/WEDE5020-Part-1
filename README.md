Project Overview
TradeLink is a proposed online platform designed to connect members of the public with qualified and reliable skilled tradespeople. The platform focuses on trades such as electricians, plumbers, builders and carpenters.
Finding a reliable tradesperson can sometimes be difficult, particularly when customers do not know whether a worker has the appropriate skills or qualifications. TradeLink aims to provide a central platform where information about tradespeople can be organised and made available to potential clients.
The website will allow visitors to browse different categories of traders and identify the type of professional they require. The long-term goal of TradeLink is to collect relevant information about registered tradespeople, including their qualifications, skills and service locations.
As the project develops, TradeLink could use a client's location and required service to help connect them with an appropriate available tradesperson in their area.
The initial version of the website focuses on providing a simple and accessible interface containing information about TradeLink, available trader categories, contact options and a gallery.

Website Goals and Objectives
The main goal of the TradeLink website is to create an online platform that makes it easier for customers to find information about qualified tradespeople.
The objectives of the website are to:
1.	Create an online presence for TradeLink.
2.	Provide clear information about TradeLink and its purpose.
3.	Allow visitors to browse different categories of skilled workers.
4.	Help customers identify the type of tradesperson required for a particular job.
5.	Provide an easy method for customers to contact TradeLink.
6.	Display examples of services through a gallery.
7.	Build trust between customers and tradespeople.
8.	Create a website that can be expanded with more advanced functionality in the future.
9.	Eventually provide location-based matching between customers and suitable tradespeople.

Key Features and Functionality
Home Page
The Home page will introduce visitors to TradeLink and provide a brief explanation of the organisation. It will contain the TradeLink logo, navigation links and a link directing users to the Traders page.
About Us Page
The About Us page will provide information about TradeLink, including its background, mission and vision.
Traders Page
The Traders page will display the different types of skilled workers available through TradeLink.
The initial categories are:
1.	Electricians
2.	Plumbers
3.	Builders
4.	Carpenters
Future versions of the website could allow customers to search and filter traders according to their location, qualifications, services and availability.
Contact Page
The Contact page will provide a form where visitors can enter their name, email address and message to contact TradeLink.
Gallery Page
The Gallery page will provide a space for images relating to TradeLink and work completed by tradespeople.
Navigation
All main pages will be connected through hyperlinks, allowing users to navigate easily between the Home, About Us, Traders, Contact and Gallery pages.
